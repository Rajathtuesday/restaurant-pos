What you get
Made for volume, not for tables.

Everything below is switched on when your outlet is set up for quick service.

Token billing
Bill at the counter and print the token on the receipt. Numbers restart every day, and online orders get their own O- series.

Kitchen tickets, automatically

03
Billing by section
Running a counter with separate sections, like a food court? One order can be billed per section.

More than one outlet
One owner login, every outlet.

Each outlet keeps its own menu prices, stock, tokens and reports. You see all of them from one login.

Outlets can raise a requisition to the central kitchen, the kitchen sends a batch, and the outlet scans it in. Stock leaves one place and arrives at the other, so the books match.

The Growth plan covers up to three outlets. Running more than that? Message me and we'll work it out.

Being straight with you
What to know before you switch.
  • Short internet drops are handled. Orders, and bills paid in cash, are saved on the device and sync when the connection comes back. UPI and card payments need the internet to confirm, and Rasova isn't built to run for hours with no connection, so keep a phone hotspot as backup.
  • The Order Ready board is marked by staff, not by kitchen sensors. Someone taps the order ready when it's handed over. It's quick, but it's a step.
  • Delivery-app orders: tell me which apps you use before you switch, and I'll tell you honestly how they fit in today.
