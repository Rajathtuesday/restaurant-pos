What you get
Everything between the door and the bill.

Everything below is switched on when your outlet is set up for dine-in.

01
Waiters order from their phones
Take the order at the table and send it. No running to a terminal, no handwritten slips the kitchen can't read.
02
Tickets to the right station
The kitchen screen and printer get the order the moment it's sent. Each kitchen station can have its own printer.
03
Modifiers that reach the cook
Less spicy, no onion, extra cheese. The note travels with the item onto the kitchen ticket.
04
Merge and split
Merge tables for big groups. Split bills by item, equally, or take part payments. Each split prints as a proper bill.
05
Guests can call a waiter
Guests browsing the QR menu can tap Call Waiter, and the staff screen shows which table needs someone.
06
Reservations and guest history
Take bookings and remember regulars: visits, spend and loyalty. Part of the Growth plan.
After service
The night closes itself.

At closing, the Z-report shows GST collected, the cash and UPI split and item-wise sales. Stock used tonight is already deducted through your recipes.

Each person sees only what their role needs: owner, manager, cashier, waiter or chef. A waiter can't give a discount they aren't allowed to.

At month end, export the GSTR-1 workbook for your CA.

Being straight with you
What to know before you switch.
  • Rasova needs internet to bill. Keep a phone hotspot ready as backup. Offline billing is on my list, and it isn't built yet.
  • CRM, loyalty and reservations come with the Growth plan, not the Solo plan.
  • Switching software mid-season is real work. Your staff need an afternoon with it, and your old reports stay in your old system. I'll help you set up, but plan the switch for a quieter week.
