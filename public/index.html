Every feature was built with the Indian restaurant workflow in mind - GST, KOT, aggregators, split bills, cash reconciliation.

🗺️
Live Floor Plan
Color-coded table states visible to every staff member in real time. Green means free. Red means occupied. No confusion during rush hour.
🧾
GST-Compliant Billing
CGST/SGST breakdown per item. GSTIN and FSSAI printed on every bill. PDF download. WhatsApp delivery. Your CA will thank you.
👨‍🍳
Kitchen Order Tickets
Orders reach the kitchen instantly. Race-condition safe - no duplicate KOTs even when ten waiters fire orders simultaneously.
📱
QR Digital Menu
Customer scans the table QR, browses the menu, adds to cart - exactly like Zomato. Works on any phone, no app download needed.
📦
Inventory Tracking
Stock deducted automatically on every KOT. Low-stock alerts. Ingredient-level recipe mapping. Know what's running out before service starts.
📊
Reports & Z-Report
Daily Z-report: GST collected, payment split between cash and UPI, item-wise sales. CSV export. Everything your accountant needs.
💳
Split Bills & Partial Pay
Split by item, split equally, partial payments, voids, refunds. Every edge case handled. No awkward moments at checkout.
🏪
Multi-Outlet Support
Manage multiple outlets under one account. Each outlet fully isolated. One login to see everything across all your locations.
👥
Role-Based Access
Owner, Manager, Waiter, Cashier, Chef - each sees exactly what they need. Nothing more. Security built into the architecture.
